Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Scope and data access

This policy covers our informational website and private owner-operated automation tool. With authorization from the channel owner, the tool uses YouTube API Services to access channel identifiers and metadata, video metadata and visibility, and aggregate analytics such as views, watch time and subscriber counts. OAuth credentials authenticate API requests; we do not request Google passwords.

Use and storage

Data is used to manage approved video uploads, verify channel identity, prevent duplicate uploads and review performance. Credentials are stored locally by the operator and in private GitHub Actions Secrets. Upload identifiers, status and file fingerprints are recorded in a private repository. The public site has no analytics scripts, forms or sign-in.

Sharing and Google data

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or transfer it to AI models for training. Use of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Retention and revocation

To revoke access, use Google account permissions. The operator can remove stored credentials and disable workflows. Authorized data will be deleted within 30 days of a deletion request or loss of authorization, subject to legal obligations.
